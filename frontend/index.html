Know where your herbs come from

AyurTrace records every step of an Ayurvedic raw material's journey — from the farmer's field to the shop shelf — on a blockchain. Nobody can quietly edit the history, so you can check a product's origin, lab test and handling before you trust it.

The journey

🌱 Farmer harvests → 🔬 Lab tests purity → ⚙️ Processor dries/grinds → 🚚 Distributor transports → 🏪 Retailer sells

Farmers, labs, processors, distributors and retailers are registered by an admin and sign each step with their own wallet.
